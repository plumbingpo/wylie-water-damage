Dealing With Roof Leak Water Damage in Wylie

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. Our team maps everything that got wet, then dries it.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. We work on the interior, which means removing wet insulation, drying drywall and framing, dealing with mold and rebuilding the ceiling. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Catch the DripsPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Protect the floor with plastic sheeting or old towels.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneA wet roof and an unlit attic are both places to stay out of. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Photograph the DamageTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.

The Usual Culprits

Why This Happens

  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • A skylight whose curb or seal has dried and cracked, so rain gets past it.
  • Sun-cracked rubber collars where plumbing vent pipes come through the roof.
  • Water backing up at the eave and under the shingles because the gutters are blocked.
  • Storm impact, such as hail or a tree branch, breaking through the shingles.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Drywall Ceilings

A ceiling panel left damp will discolor, bow and weaken. The tape releases along cracked seams, and with enough weight a section can come down suddenly.

Hidden Attic Mold

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Attic Insulation

Wet insulation mats down, stops insulating and holds water against the ceiling drywall for days. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Cellulose that is saturated generally comes out.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Because fixtures are holes in the drywall, a leak often shows up at a light or fan first. Keep that circuit off until someone has inspected it, since damp wiring may corrode or short out.

Will Insurance Pay?

Does Insurance Cover It?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. A leak put down to an old, worn or neglected roof is usually denied, and fixing the roof may be treated separately from the interior work. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Cost

What Affects the Cost

  • How long the roof leaked before it was found, and any mold growth.
  • Drywall removal: how much of the ceiling and walls is beyond saving.
  • How much repainting is required so the patched ceiling blends in.
  • Whether water got past the ceiling into walls, flooring or belongings below.
