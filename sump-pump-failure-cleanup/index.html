Dealing With Sump Pump Failure Cleanup in Wylie

The moment a basement most needs its sump pump is often the moment the pump gives out. Groundwater rises in a big storm and pours into the pit, right as the electricity fails, the float jams or the inflow outruns the pump. The pit overflows and the basement floor starts to flood. It keeps coming until a pump is running again.

Clean is the wrong word for what comes out of a sump pit. Soil, silt, bacteria and the contents of the drain tile travel with it, so our team rates it no better than Category 2, and Category 3 when it has stood for a while or sewage is involved. Cleanup is different as a result, with wet drywall, carpet pad and other porous materials generally removed and the rest cleaned and disinfected ahead of drying.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Keep Out of the BasementWith the electricity still on, a flooded basement is not safe to walk into. Outlets, the pump cord or the furnace might already be wet and live.
  2. Shut Off Basement PowerIf the breaker panel can be reached while you stand somewhere dry, switch off the circuits for the basement. If the panel is in the water, call the utility.
  3. Check the Pump SafelyLook to see whether the pump came unplugged, its outlet tripped or the float is pinned against the pit wall. Do not reach into the water.
  4. Assume the Water Is DirtyWear rubber boots and gloves if you must go down, keep kids and pets upstairs, and wash your hands after touching anything wet.
  5. Move Valuables and CallCarry dry valuables upstairs, photograph the water level and the pump, then call us. Do not wait for the water to drain away by itself.
How We Fix It

What Happens After You Call

1

Get the Water Out

We bring our own pumps and extractors, so nothing depends on your failed sump. We clear silt and standing water off the floor, out of low areas and from around the pit.

2

Remove Contaminated Materials

Our team wears protective gear to remove wet drywall, insulation, carpet and pad reached by groundwater, then bags and hauls it off, leaving bare framing and concrete.

3

Wash and Disinfect

We wash the framing, the slab and the contents that are staying, then apply an antimicrobial. Drying comes afterward, because fans on a dirty basement would blow contaminants everywhere.

4

Dry and Verify

Dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture meter readings on the framing and concrete return to normal, typically 3 to 5 days. Make sure a functioning pump is installed again by the time drying wraps up.

Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

Few water claims confuse homeowners as much as this one. Water backing up or overflowing from a sump is usually excluded under a standard homeowners policy, and a flood policy is a different product with rules of its own. Often the only coverage comes from an added endorsement for water backup and sump overflow, which has limits of its own. Check the declarations page for it, or call your agent to find out. We record the pump failure, the water level and the damage so your claim file is complete.

Why It Happens

Why This Happens

  • A tripped outlet or breaker, or a plug knocked loose, leaving the pump without power.
  • A pump too small for the volume of water entering the pit.
  • Check valve trouble, which makes the pump move the same water again and again.
  • Stuck or snagged float switches, which never tell the pump to start.
  • Dirt and small rocks washed into the basin that lock up the pump.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Bottom Plates and Studs

The wood plate at the base of each wall sits right on the slab and soaks up water first. With the wall opened up, this wood is scrubbed, treated and then dried to normal readings.

Drywall and Wall Insulation

The wet zone inside a wall reaches well past the water mark, as drywall and insulation pull moisture up off the floor. Because the water is contaminated, the wet lower section is cut out, not dried.

Basement Floor Coverings

Groundwater-soaked carpet and padding come out, as cleaning cannot make them safe. Silty water also gets caught below vinyl plank and laminate, which normally means lifting them.

Furnace and Water Heater

The furnace and water heater frequently sit on the same floor as the pit. Water reaching burners, controls or wiring can ruin them, and each appliance must be inspected before it is used again.
