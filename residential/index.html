Extraction Services

Residential Water Extraction Services

When water gets into your home from a burst pipe, a failed appliance, or storm flooding, professional extraction is the safest and fastest option. From a small leak to a fully flooded floor, Wylie Restoration removes water from homes across Wylie. On each job we assess the damage, pump out the water with industrial equipment, map the moisture, protect your belongings, and document everything for your insurer.

Water left sitting indoors threatens the house itself, everything in it, and the people who live there. It takes only hours for drywall to swell, hardwood to warp, and electrical components to start corroding. If left untreated for 24 to 48 hours, mold growth becomes almost inevitable. Our advice is simple: pick up the phone as soon as you find water pooling indoors, no matter how little there is. Our technicians can assess the extent of the damage and begin extraction before secondary damage sets in.

Not every job starts with a flood, and some come from a concealed leak that has run for days or even weeks. Long before anything shows on the surface, a slow drip under flooring or inside a wall can do serious harm to the structure. To track down trapped moisture and verify everything is dry, our crew relies on thermal cameras and professional-grade meters.

Drying Services

Moisture Monitoring and Structural Drying

After water gets in, nothing matters more for the long-term health of your house than drying the structure all the way through. Done right, it pulls trapped moisture out of subfloors, wall cavities, and framing, keeps mold from starting, and returns the structure to sound condition. We take and log moisture readings daily, and each Wylie Restoration drying job is run according to IICRC S500.

For homes, we bring commercial-grade dehumidifiers and fast air movers, add specialty equipment where hardwood flooring or wall cavities need it, and use thermal imaging to confirm progress along the way. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians calibrate the drying plan to match your property's specific materials, layout, and damage severity.

Many of our customers do not realize at first how much work a proper dry-out takes. Even after visible water is gone, moisture trapped in wall cavities, under flooring, and in structural framing can fuel mold growth and cause wood rot for weeks. We monitor moisture levels daily and don't consider the job complete until every reading reaches pre-loss standards. Expect 3 to 5 days for a typical house, with the exact time set by how far the damage goes.

Stopping Mold

Mold Prevention and Remediation for Homes

Give damp materials 24 to 48 hours and mold can take hold, then move fast through the house and seriously threaten your household's health. Wylie Restoration provides professional mold prevention as part of every water damage restoration project, as well as standalone mold remediation for existing growth. For mold work, our IICRC-certified team sticks to the EPA guidance and the IICRC S520 standard so the job is both safe and complete.

Any time a home has water damage, our advice is to include a mold prevention treatment. It works better and costs less to apply antimicrobials while the house is drying than to deal with established mold later. However, if you already have visible mold or suspect hidden mold from a previous water event, we provide comprehensive testing, containment, removal, and post-remediation verification to ensure your home is safe and healthy.

Sewage Cleanup

Safe Sewage Cleanup in Your Home

Sewage backups are among the most hazardous water damage events a homeowner can face. Category 3 black water contains bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens that pose serious health risks. Wylie Restoration technicians are specially trained and equipped to handle sewage cleanup safely, following strict IICRC protocols for contaminated water restoration.

We contain the affected area, extract the contaminated water safely, remove what cannot be saved, apply a thorough antimicrobial treatment, dry the structure completely, and test afterward to verify the home is safe. One team does the whole job so that your family is back in a clean and healthy house without delay.
