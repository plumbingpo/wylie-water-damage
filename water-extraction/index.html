Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Wylie Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Wylie using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Add our number, (321) 524-3149, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
