Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Wylie

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
