Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.

How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
