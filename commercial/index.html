Prevention and response plans are developed together with your board, property manager, and maintenance committee so they protect the community and stay within budget. Damage Prevention

Risk Assessments That Help Prevent Commercial Water Damage

Owning commercial property comes with a duty of care, which means keeping the premises safe. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. Using thermal imaging and moisture monitoring equipment, Wylie Restoration performs professional risk assessments that check how vulnerable each area is to water getting in. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

There are two benefits here, since spotting weak points keeps the property and its occupants safer, while documented due diligence may help you legally after an incident. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. We recommend annual moisture assessments for all commercial properties, with more frequent inspections for high-risk areas such as basements, mechanical rooms, and aging plumbing systems.
